# Evidence, not assumptions

## Treatment
**Format:** 1:1 square, 30 seconds, six scenes, no narration.
**Promise:** A recruiter can inspect Layla Drummond's SOC L1 evidence, not just a skills list.
**Identity:** The existing portfolio's graphite (#121014), plum (#291c28), hot pink (#ff62ad), off-white (#f8eff5), Manrope and DM Mono. No third-party logos or fictional person imagery.
**Hero:** A pink evidence marker that becomes an investigation focus, an email seal, a coaching connection, and a cited answer. A real screenshot anchors the website reveal.
**Motion:** Masked kinetic typography, perspective assembly, SVG path tracing, matched pink-circle transformations. A persistent evidence marker has one owner and explicit positions for all six scenes.
**Arc:** A fast evidence hook; slower investigation proof; mechanical triage; human communication; an interface reveal with honest abstention; a pink-field signature.
**Payoff:** Switch from dark evidence surfaces to a full pink identity lockup, with LinkedIn messaging as the only public contact.
**Source:** ../../design/verified-evidence.md and the actual portfolio. Numbers are resume-reported; lab claims are always labeled. No fictional logs, incidents, screenshots, URL or business outcomes. Chat artwork is a labeled illustrative excerpt, not a recorded conversation.

## Pre-code review
Six compositions differ in palette, focus, scale and action: kinetic word stage, numeric investigation record, transforming envelope, asymmetric coaching bridge, real product interface, inverted identity card. Every main scene has meaningful post-entrance action and an active last 15–20%. Large type, 7% safe margins, no full-field empty tails, no random particles, no flashing. Claims are sourced and labeled; contact excludes private details. Native SVG/CSS transforms only; no WebGL dependency.
